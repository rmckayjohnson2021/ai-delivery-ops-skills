# Portfolio Positioning

## Portfolio Role

This repo is the delivery-method companion to two implementation-focused repos:

- `team-ai-incident-triage` shows a concrete AI workflow for incident triage.
- `llm-cost-eval-gateway` shows reusable execution controls for model calls, cost, routing, retries, and evaluation.
- `ai-delivery-ops-skills` shows the repeatable planning system that helps teams create reviewable, measurable, governance-aware AI delivery artifacts.

## Employer Message

I build AI delivery systems that teams can reuse, evaluate, govern, and hand off for implementation.

## What Reviewers Should Notice

- The skill pack turns ambiguous requests into concrete artifacts.
- Evaluation and governance are first-class, not afterthoughts.
- The repo is reusable across multiple AI workflow projects.
- The artifacts are practical enough for engineering handoff, not just strategy.

## Boundaries

This repo does not claim that generated artifacts are automatically correct. It helps structure the work so humans can review decisions, fill gaps, and approve the next phase.

It also does not replace the companion repos. Instead, it explains how similar projects should be planned, evaluated, and governed.
